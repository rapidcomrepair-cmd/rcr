RCR Shop – Privacy Policy

Rapid Computer Repair, Fort Worth, TX · Last updated November 19, 2026

RCR Shop is a private tool used by Rapid Computer Repair to manage its own repair tickets, customers and invoices. It is not offered to the public, and only the shop's own Google account can sign in to use it.

What the app accesses

When the shop owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to use the owner's Google Drive. It reads and saves the shop's own files in a single Drive folder (Documents › RCR): the invoice log, invoices, and the dashboard's ticket data. It does not read, change or share any other files.

If the owner turns on "Check Gmail", the app also asks for read-only access to the owner's Gmail. It only searches for Amazon and eBay order emails, reads them in the owner's browser to fill in parts orders (item, price, order number, delivery date, tracking), and keeps nothing else. It cannot send, change or delete email, and email content is never sent anywhere except between Google and the owner's device. Access can be removed at any time in the Google account (Security › Third-party access).

If the owner turns on "iPhone Calendar", the app asks for permission to make and manage its own calendar ("RCR Appointments") in the owner's Google Calendar and keeps the shop's appointments there. It cannot see or change any other calendar.

Where data is kept

All shop data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's own devices (the browser's local storage, so the app can work while the connection drops). This website only delivers the app itself; it has no server or database and never receives, stores or sells any data. Nothing is shared with third parties, and there are no ads or tracking.

Google sign-in

Sign-in uses Google's standard OAuth. The access Google grants lasts one hour and is kept only in the owner's browser. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Customer information

Customer names, phone numbers and repair details are entered by the shop for its own records and invoices, and are handled the same way as the shop's other business records.
